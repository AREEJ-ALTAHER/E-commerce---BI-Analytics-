# Dashboard Guide

## 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of business performance.

### KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Profit Margin
- Average Order Value

### Visualizations
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Monthly Profit Trend

---

## 2. Product Performance

Analyzes product-level sales and profitability.

### Visualizations
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Sales vs Profit by Product

---

## 3. Customer Performance

Analyzes customer segments, activity, sales, and profitability.

### Visualizations
- Profit by Customer Segment
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Customer Sales vs Order Frequency
- Customer Activity Status
- Profit Margin by Customer Segment

---

## 4. Regional Performance

Analyzes geographical sales and profitability.

### Visualizations
- Sales by State
- Profit by State
- Geographic Sales Distribution
- Top 15 States by Profit Margin
- Sales vs Profit by State
